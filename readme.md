## Speed Typing Mavis

A modern, minimalist typing game to beat the clock by typing random words with speed and precision.

## Features & Enhancements

- **Clean Minimalist UI**: Sleek matte aesthetic with warm highlights, refined container card, and crisp modern typography (`Inter` + `JetBrains Mono`).
- **Dynamic Theme Switcher**: Toggle between 3 distinct functional themes:
  - *Neo-Dark* (Monkeytype / Linear matte aesthetic - default)
  - *Cyberpunk* (Neon velocity cyan & magenta highlights)
  - *Paper Minimal* (Clean warm daylight light mode)
- **Interactive HUD**: Real-time counter with urgent low-time pulse alerts (`≤ 3s`) and live score tracker.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Smooth word pop animations, typo shake feedback, and custom settings dropdowns.
- **Difficulty Modes**: Easy (+5s bonus), Medium (+3s bonus), and Hard (+2s bonus), persisted in `localStorage`.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> on Game Over to instantly play again, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> to dismiss settings.
